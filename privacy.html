Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

MyShiftPay is designed to help users track work shifts, hours, earnings, and related work information.

Data Collection

MyShiftPay does not collect, sell, or share personal data. Information entered into the app, including work hours, pay rates, tips, extras, company and position names, is stored only on your device. The app does not automatically send this information to the developer or to anyone else. The app does not require an account.

Third Parties, Tracking and Advertising

MyShiftPay does not send user data to third parties. Information leaves your device only when you choose to send it yourself (see Copy and Share), and purchases are handled by Apple (see In-App Purchases). MyShiftPay does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking technologies.

In-App Purchases

MyShiftPay offers optional in-app purchases (Lifetime Pro and tips). Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store under Apple's terms and privacy policy. MyShiftPay does not receive or store your payment information. To show your purchase status, the app checks your purchases with Apple on your device and saves the result locally. This information is not sent to the developer.

Copy and Share

When you use Copy or Share, your weekly summary is copied to your clipboard or sent through the app you choose. MyShiftPay does not send it anywhere else.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can edit or delete individual entries in the app, or remove all app data by deleting MyShiftPay from your iPhone. Because the developer does not hold any of your data, there is nothing to request deletion of. Your iPhone or iCloud backups may include app data; these backups are managed by Apple under your device settings.

Children's Privacy

MyShiftPay does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
