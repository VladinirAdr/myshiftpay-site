Contact us

Found a bug, have a question, or want to suggest an improvement? Write to us:

support@myshiftpayapp.com

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro and tips are optional one-time purchases. Everything else in MyShiftPay is free.

New iPhone or reinstalled the app? Open Settings → Support MyShiftPay → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Lifetime Pro.

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

MyShiftPay stores your work data only on your iPhone and does not require an account. There is no cloud sync: deleting the app deletes your data. Your iPhone or iCloud backup may include it.
