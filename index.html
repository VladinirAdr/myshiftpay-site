MyShiftPay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

MyShiftPay is designed to help users track work shifts, hours, earnings, and related work information.

Data Collection

MyShiftPay does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. Information entered into the app is stored locally on the user's device.

Third-Party Tracking and Advertising

MyShiftPay does not use advertising networks or third-party tracking technologies.

Data Storage

App data is stored locally on the user's device and remains under the user's control. Removing the app or its local data may remove information stored by the app.

Children's Privacy

MyShiftPay does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support information provided on the MyShiftPay App Store listing.
